Enemy Base Prompt

Read First:

- /art/STYLE.md
- /art/AGENTS.md

Purpose:

Base generation template for enemy assets used in Mahjong Three Kingdom TD.

This template defines the visual foundation for enemy units and hostile battlefield characters.
Specific enemy faction, class, weapon, and role must come from queue tasks.

---

Style Requirements

Visual Style:

- Cute Premium
- Stylized 2.5D
- Leaning toward 2D illustration
- Chibi military fantasy
- Readable hostile identity

Must match all STYLE.md requirements.

---

Enemy Identity

Enemies should feel:

- Distinct from player units
- Readable at gameplay scale
- Tactically recognizable
- Memorable without becoming grotesque

Avoid:

- Gore
- Horror design
- Hyper-monster realism
- Dark grim realism

Hostile does not mean disturbing.

---

Proportion Rules

Character Direction:

- Chibi
- 2.5 to 3.5 heads tall
- Strong silhouette
- Clear expression
- Clear equipment identity

Avoid:

- Realistic proportions
- Heavy grotesque mutation
- Tiny unreadable detail clusters

---

Rendering Requirements

Rendering:

- Soft lighting
- Mild shadow volume
- Illustration-first readability
- Strong shape clarity

Avoid:

- Photorealism
- Plastic material look
- Heavy texture noise
- Muddy color separation

---

Background Rules

Required:

- Transparent background
- PNG-ready asset
- Fully isolated subject

Forbidden:

- White background
- Colored background
- Battlefield scene
- Decorative environment

Asset must require ZERO manual background removal.

---

Composition Rules

- Single enemy per asset
- Full body preferred
- Centered composition
- Combat role readable from silhouette

Avoid:

- Multi-enemy composition
- Cropped body
- Busy environment staging
- Extreme perspective distortion

---

Export Rules

Output Type:

- Individual asset
- Game-ready
- Transparent PNG compatible

Asset should be suitable for:

- Battle placement
- Enemy codex UI
- Stage encounter previews

No post-processing required.

---

Consistency Rules

Must follow:

- STYLE.md
- AGENTS.md
- Chibi readability rules
- Project-wide visual language

No style drift allowed.

---

Queue Integration

Enemy identity will be supplied by queue tasks.

Examples:

- enemy_yellowturban_spearman_v1
- enemy_bandit_raider_v1
- enemy_warlord_guard_v1

This template only defines the visual foundation.
