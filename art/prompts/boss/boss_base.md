Boss Base Prompt

Read First:

- /art/STYLE.md
- /art/AGENTS.md

Purpose:

Base generation template for boss assets used in Mahjong Three Kingdom TD.

This template defines the visual foundation for boss enemies, elite commanders, and large encounter leaders.
Specific boss identity, faction, chapter role, and combat theme must come from queue tasks.

---

Style Requirements

Visual Style:

- Cute Premium
- Stylized 2.5D
- Leaning toward 2D illustration
- Chibi military fantasy
- Memorable encounter presence

Must match all STYLE.md requirements.

---

Boss Identity

Bosses should feel:

- Important
- High-threat
- Visually memorable
- More dramatic than standard enemies

Bosses must still remain:

- Chibi
- Readable
- Warm-toned within project style
- Suitable for tactical gameplay visibility

Avoid:

- Horror boss design
- Gore
- Full realism
- Unreadable oversized clutter

---

Proportion Rules

Character Direction:

- Chibi-based proportions
- Slightly more imposing silhouette than standard units
- Strong facial expression
- Clear weapon, armor, or emblem identity

Avoid:

- Giant realism
- Hyper-detailed noise
- Anatomy realism that breaks project style

---

Rendering Requirements

Rendering:

- Soft lighting
- Mild shadow volume
- Clear heroic-villain readability
- Strong silhouette emphasis

Avoid:

- Photorealism
- Heavy metal realism
- Plastic material look
- Dark muddy rendering

---

Background Rules

Required:

- Transparent background
- PNG-ready asset
- Fully isolated subject

Forbidden:

- White background
- Colored background
- Boss arena scene
- Decorative environment staging

Asset must require ZERO manual background removal.

---

Composition Rules

- Single boss per asset
- Full body preferred
- Centered composition
- Signature weapon or identifying feature clearly visible
- Strong readable pose

Avoid:

- Multi-character composition
- Cropped body
- Extreme perspective distortion
- Visual overload

---

Export Rules

Output Type:

- Individual asset
- Game-ready
- Transparent PNG compatible

Asset should be suitable for:

- Boss encounter screens
- Battle placement
- Stage previews
- Codex UI

No post-processing required.

---

Consistency Rules

Must follow:

- STYLE.md
- AGENTS.md
- Chibi readability rules
- Project-wide visual language

No style drift allowed.

---

Queue Integration

Boss identity will be supplied by queue tasks.

Examples:

- boss_dongzhuo_base_v1
- boss_bandit_king_v1
- boss_warlord_elite_v1

This template only defines the visual foundation.
