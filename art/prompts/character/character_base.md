Character Base Prompt

Read First:

- /art/STYLE.md
- /art/AGENTS.md

Purpose:

Base generation template for character assets used in Mahjong Three Kingdom TD.

This template supports:

- Lords
- Heroes
- Soldiers

This template defines the visual foundation only.
Specific character identity, faction, pose, and equipment must come from queue tasks.

---

Style Requirements

Visual Style:

- Cute Premium
- Stylized 2.5D
- Leaning toward 2D illustration
- Three Kingdom inspired chibi military fantasy
- Warm heroic presentation

Must match all STYLE.md requirements.

---

Character Type Support

This template must support:

Lords:

- Strong faction identity
- High recognizability
- Commanding silhouette
- Premium hero presence

Heroes:

- Distinct personality
- Readable weapon or role identity
- Clear battle silhouette
- Memorable expression

Soldiers:

- Simpler design language than heroes
- Clean class readability
- Fast visual recognition in battle
- Consistent faction-aligned military style

---

Proportion Rules

Character Direction:

- Chibi
- 2.5 to 3.5 heads tall
- Large readable head
- Clear expression
- Strong silhouette

Avoid:

- Realistic body proportions
- Tall heroic realism
- Tiny baby-like proportions
- Over-sexualized design

Characters must remain readable at gameplay scale.

---

Faction Rules

Faction identity must follow STYLE.md.

Wei:

- Navy
- Black
- Dark metal
- Order and discipline feeling

Shu:

- Green
- Warm gold
- Heroic warmth

Wu:

- Crimson
- Orange-red
- Flexible and passionate feeling

Faction color rules apply to outfit, trim, banners, symbols, and effect accents.

---

Rendering Requirements

Rendering:

- Soft lighting
- Mild shadow volume
- Slight depth and volume
- Illustration-first readability
- Clean shape separation

Avoid:

- Photorealism
- Heavy physically based rendering
- Plastic material look
- Dark gritty war realism

---

Background Rules

Required:

- Transparent background
- PNG-ready asset
- Fully isolated subject

Forbidden:

- White background
- Colored background
- Gradient background
- Battlefield scene
- Interior scene
- Decorative framing
- Background props not attached to the character

Asset must require ZERO manual background removal.

---

Composition Rules

- Single character per asset
- Full body preferred
- Centered composition
- Pose readable at small scale
- Weapon and role identity clearly visible

Avoid:

- Cropped body
- Extreme foreshortening
- Multi-character compositions
- Busy scene staging

---

Export Rules

Output Type:

- Individual asset
- Game-ready
- Transparent PNG compatible

Asset should be suitable for:

- Battle placement
- Character selection UI
- Roster UI
- Upgrade UI
- Marketing cutout reuse

No post-processing required.

---

Consistency Rules

Must follow:

- STYLE.md
- AGENTS.md
- Faction consistency rules
- Chibi readability rules
- Project-wide visual language

No style drift allowed.

---

Queue Integration

Character identity will be supplied by queue tasks.

Examples:

- lord_caocao_base_v1
- hero_guanyu_base_v1
- soldier_shu_archer_base_v1

This template only defines the visual foundation.
