Hero Base Prompt

Read First:

- /art/STYLE.md
- /art/AGENTS.md

Purpose:

Master generation template for all hero assets in Mahjong Three Kingdom TD.

This template defines the visual foundation for all playable heroes.

Specific hero identity will be supplied by queue tasks.

---

Character Style

Visual Direction:

- Cute Premium
- Three Kingdoms Fantasy
- Stylized 2.5D
- Leaning toward 2D illustration

Character Type:

- Chibi Hero
- Tactical Commander
- Battlefield General

Avoid:

- Realistic historical portrait
- Anime realism
- Hyper-detailed rendering
- Western fantasy style

---

Body Proportion

Requirements:

- 2.5 to 3.5 heads tall
- Large readable head
- Compact body
- Strong silhouette

Gameplay readability is prioritized.

Hero must remain recognizable at small battlefield scale.

---

Face Design

Requirements:

- Friendly expression
- Strong personality
- Memorable silhouette
- Distinct facial identity

Avoid:

- Generic NPC face
- Excessive realism
- Mature war drama expression

Character should feel heroic and approachable.

---

Costume Design

Theme:

Three Kingdoms military fantasy.

Materials:

- Cloth
- Leather
- Decorative armor
- Military accessories

Visual Goal:

- Heroic
- Distinctive
- Readable

Avoid:

- Heavy realism
- Full plate armor
- Modern military equipment
- Sci-fi elements

---

Faction Rules

Wei:

Palette:

- Navy
- Black
- Dark steel

Theme:

- Discipline
- Authority

---

Shu:

Palette:

- Green
- Gold

Theme:

- Loyalty
- Heroism

---

Wu:

Palette:

- Crimson
- Warm orange

Theme:

- Passion
- Flexibility

---

Rendering Requirements

Rendering Style:

- Soft lighting
- Mild shadow volume
- Clean silhouette

Visual Priority:

1. Readability
2. Personality
3. Faction Identity
4. Visual Quality

Avoid:

- Photorealism
- Heavy texture noise
- Excessive visual effects

---

Background Rules

Required:

- Transparent background
- Fully isolated character
- PNG-ready asset

Forbidden:

- Scene background
- Battlefield background
- Decorative environment
- Framing elements

Asset must require ZERO manual background removal.

---

Composition Rules

Single character only.

Centered composition.

No extra characters.

No mounts.

No pets.

No battlefield objects.

---

Export Rules

Output:

- Full-body character
- Transparent PNG
- Game-ready asset

Suitable For:

- Home UI
- Hero Collection
- Battle Deployment
- Character Profile

---

Consistency Rules

Must follow:

- STYLE.md
- AGENTS.md
- Faction rules
- Chibi proportion rules

No style drift allowed.
